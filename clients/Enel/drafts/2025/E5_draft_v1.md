## E5-1 — Policies related to resource use and circular economy

Enel has established a comprehensive set of policies addressing resource efficiency, waste hierarchy, and circular design, aligned with the EU Circular Economy Action Plan and the EU Taxonomy Regulation. The "Circular Economy Policy," approved by the Board of Directors, commits the Group to decouple economic growth from resource consumption through the principles of reduce, reuse, and recycle. This policy covers the full lifecycle of Enel’s assets—from renewable energy plants and grid infrastructure to smart meters and electric vehicle charging points—ensuring that ecodesign and circular design principles are systematically integrated into product development and procurement.

Specifically, Enel applies ecodesign principles to its core products and services. For example, wind turbine blades and solar photovoltaic panels are designed for extended durability, repairability, and end-of-life recyclability. The policy also mandates that all new grid components (transformers, cables, substations) meet minimum recycled content thresholds and are reusable or recyclable. Enel explicitly aligns its resource efficiency scope with the EU’s Waste Framework Directive and the European Green Deal’s circular economy objectives, and reports annually against the EU Taxonomy criteria for circular economy.
