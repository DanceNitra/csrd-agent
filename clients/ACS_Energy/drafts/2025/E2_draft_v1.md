## E2-2 — Actions and resources related to pollution

ACS Energy has implemented actionable pollution reduction plans for each major pollutant type. For air emissions, the three thermal power plants are equipped with flue gas desulfurization systems (reducing SOx), selective catalytic reduction (SCR) for NOx, and electrostatic precipitators for particulate matter. The gas-fired peaker plants use low-NOx burners. For water pollution, each thermal plant operates a closed-loop cooling water system and a wastewater treatment plant that removes heavy metals and other contaminants before discharge. Soil contamination prevention is addressed through leak detection systems and groundwater monitoring at fuel storage and ash disposal areas. Hazardous substances—including oils, solvents, and chemicals used in water treatment—are managed under a REACH-compliant inventory and replaced where feasible with safer alternatives.

The company has published a five-year investment roadmap (2024–2028) committing an additional €45 million for advanced pollution abatement technologies, including carbon capture integration studies and further improvements to water discharge quality.
