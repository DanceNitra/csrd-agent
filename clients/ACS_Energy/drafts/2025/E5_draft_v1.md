## E5-1 — Policies related to resource use and circular economy

ACS Energy has established a comprehensive circular economy policy framework aligned with the EU Circular Economy Action Plan and the EU Taxonomy Regulation. These policies govern resource efficiency across our electricity generation, transmission, and distribution operations, covering all three countries of operation (Slovakia, Czech Republic, Germany). The policies explicitly address the waste hierarchy (prevention, reuse, recycling, recovery, disposal) and incorporate ecodesign principles for our key physical assets, including turbine blades, transformers, and solar panels.

Our circular economy policies require that all new equipment procurement and facility upgrades consider durability, repairability, and recyclability. For example, in our wind turbine fleet we apply component refurbishment programs (blades and gearboxes), and our thermal power plants have implemented coal ash recycling for construction materials. These ecodesign and circular design principles are embedded in our asset lifecycle management procedures. The policies also include supplier requirements for circular material sourcing, ensuring that vendors meet minimum recycled content thresholds and provide take-back options for end-of-life components.

The policy framework is formally approved by the Board of Directors and is reviewed annually. It is integrated into our Environmental Management System (ISO 14001 certified across all sites) and cross-referenced with our climate change mitigation policies (E1) and pollution prevention policies (E2). Key performance indicators under these policies include the circular material use rate, waste diversion rate, and recycled content in packaging. ## E5-2 — Actions and resources related to circular economy

ACS Energy is implementing a structured Circular Economy Action Plan covering the period 2024–2028, with allocated CAPEX and OPEX resources. The plan focuses on three core areas: material substitution, end-of-life component recovery, and supplier circularity. In 2024, we allocated €4.2 million in CAPEX and €1.8 million in OPEX to circular economy initiatives, including a transformer oil recovery and reconditioning facility, expansion of our coal ash recycling program, and pilot projects for solar panel refurbishment.

Material substitution programs aim to reduce virgin material consumption in our operations. For instance, we have replaced virgin copper with recycled copper in certain transformer windings and use recycled steel in wind turbine tower flanges. Take-back programs are operational for used transformer oil (100% recovery), spent lubricants, and end-of-life turbine blades. Our refurbishment and remanufacturing program for turbine components has extended the service life of 8 blades and 12 gearboxes in the reporting year, avoiding approximately 45 tonnes of composite waste.

Supplier engagement for circular material sourcing is embedded in our procurement contracts. All major suppliers of metals, lubricants, and electrical components must report the recycled content of delivered materials and provide a circularity plan for packaging. ## E5-3 — Targets related to resource use and circular economy

ACS Energy has set quantitative circular economy targets with 2022 as the base year. These targets are integrated into our sustainability performance scorecard and reviewed by the Management Board quarterly. The key targets include:

- **Circular material use rate**: Increase from 6.5% (2024 actual) to 12% by 2030.
- **Waste reduction**: Reduce total waste generated per GWh of electricity produced by 20% by 2030 (2022 baseline: 2.4 tonnes/GWh).
- **Hazardous waste reduction**: Reduce hazardous waste generation by 30% by 2030 (2022 baseline: 4,800 tonnes).
- **Recycled content in products**: Achieve 20% recycled content in key metal components (transformers, switchgear) by 2030.
- **Virgin material reduction**: Reduce virgin material input (non-renewable) by 10% by 2030 (2022 baseline: 1.8 million tonnes).
- **Waste diversion from landfill**: Increase waste diversion rate from 54% (2024) to 75% by 2030.

## E5-5 — Waste metrics

ACS Energy generated 28,500 tonnes of waste in 2024, of which 4,200 tonnes (14.7%) were classified as hazardous (e.g., transformer oils, contaminated soils, asbestos from demolition) and 24,300 tonnes (85.3%) as non-hazardous (mainly coal ash, scrap metal, wood, and packaging). The waste diversion rate from disposal reached 54%, comprising 38% recycling, 10% recovery (including energy recovery from waste oils and biomass residues), and 6% reuse (refurbished components). Waste sent to landfill accounted for 18% (5,130 tonnes), and waste sent to incineration (with energy recovery) represented 28% (7,980 tonnes). Landfill waste primarily consists of non-recyclable coal ash fractions and inert construction debris.

Waste intensity was 23.75 tonnes per €1 million revenue (28,500 tonnes / €1,200 million). This metric will be tracked in future reports to monitor decoupling from revenue growth. Waste recovery methods include: recycling (scrap metal sent to smelters, cardboard to paper mills, coal ash to cement plants), energy recovery (waste oils co-processed in cement kilns, biomass residues burned in fluidised bed boilers), and landfilling (non-hazardous inert waste at licensed sites).
