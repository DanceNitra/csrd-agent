Consolidated sustainability statement prepared in accordance with ESRS as mandated by CSRD (EU 2022/2464). Reporting scope covers BASF SE and all consolidated entities globally. Prepared on a 'double materiality' basis following ESRS 2 (IRO-1, IRO-2). The sustainability statement is integrated into the BASF Annual Report and applies the Verbund integrated production model as the foundational operating principle. BASF's value chain spans the full chemical production spectrum under the Verbund integrated production system. Upstream: raw materials including naphtha, natural gas, and renewable feedstocks sourced globally. Own operations: 6 Verbund sites (Ludwigshafen, Antwerp, Freeport, Geismar, Kuantan, Nanjing) and multiple standalone production facilities across Europe, Asia, and the Americas. Downstream: sales to automotive, construction, agriculture, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and consumer goods industries. Logistics via marine, rail, and pipeline networks integrated across Verbund sites. The Verbund system enables by-product and energy cascading between production plants, significantly reducing resource consumption and emissions compared to standalone operations. BASF maintains continuous stakeholder engagement through: employees (works councils across European sites, global employee survey, collective bargaining agreements with IG BCE); investors (Capital Markets Day, quarterly earnings calls, ESG roadshows, Annual General Meeting); customers (sustainability assessments, ChemCycling partnerships, circular economy co-innovation); suppliers (Responsible Care, Together for Sustainability supply chain audits, supplier code of conduct); regulators (EU chemicals regulation REACH, German Federal Environment Agency, national authorities across 80+ countries); local communities (community advisory panels at major Verbund sites, Ludwigshafen citizen dialogue); NGOs (dialogue with environmental organizations on biodiversity, chemical safety, circular economy); academia (cooperative research, innovation partnerships). Double materiality assessment conducted annually in accordance with ESRS 2 methodology.
